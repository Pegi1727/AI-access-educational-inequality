## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** Why do you rely on free AI models rather than advanced commercial tiers?

**Participant:** The primary reason is systemic financial isolation; international credit cards are non-functional here, making official purchase impossible.

**Interviewer:** How does this banking isolation impact your academic workflow?

**Participant:** It forces us to rely on free tiers that come with heavy rate limits, truncated outputs, and restricted contextual comprehension.

**Interviewer:** What is the consequence of these technical limits on your academic writing?

**Participant:** Text cohesion suffers because the free tool cannot remember context across extended paragraphs, requiring constant manual patching.

**Interviewer:** How does this technological gap influence global academic competition?

**Participant:** It widens the divide between well-resourced global institutions and researchers working under sanctioned infrastructural constraints.

**Interviewer:** Do you feel that international academic communities understand these constraints?

**Participant:** Generally no; the assumption in global academia is that everyone can simply subscribe to whatever digital tool they need.

**Interviewer:** How does your experience align with the goals of SDG 4?

**Participant:** It emphasizes that inclusive and equitable quality education cannot exist while technological embargos isolate academic communities.
