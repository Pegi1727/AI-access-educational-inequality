## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How does using free AI tools shape your perception of academic equity?

**Participant:** It reinforces the feeling of operating as peripheral scholars who must overcome structural obstacles that central academic institutions never face.

**Interviewer:** What barriers prevent you from upgrading to premium versions?

**Participant:** The global financial blockade means local currency cannot be converted or used for international software subscriptions.

**Interviewer:** How does this restriction manifest in your scientific manuscript drafts?

**Participant:** The discourse cohesion remains fragile; the free tool often introduces unnatural phrasing that requires careful manual correction.

**Interviewer:** Do you feel that reviewers evaluate your writing fairly?

**Participant:** Reviewers often critique 'awkward phrasing' without realizing that native-level AI polishing tools are inaccessible to us due to sanctions.

**Interviewer:** How does this affect collaborative international research?

**Participant:** It creates an unstated inequality where researchers in sanctioned settings are relegated to secondary support roles.

**Interviewer:** What is essential for achieving SDG 4 in your view?

**Participant:** True educational resilience requires dismantling geopolitical barriers to learning technologies and scientific software.
