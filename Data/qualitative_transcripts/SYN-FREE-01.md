## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How would you describe your experience using freely available AI tools for academic writing?

**Participant:** Free models are helpful for basic grammar correction, but they frequently produce repetitive phrasing and lose context over longer sections.

**Interviewer:** What challenges do you encounter when trying to upgrade to advanced AI tiers?

**Participant:** We are completely blocked from purchasing subscriptions due to international banking sanctions and the absence of foreign payment methods.

**Interviewer:** How does relying on free tiers affect your academic self-perception compared to global peers?

**Participant:** You constantly feel a sense of digital FOMO, knowing that researchers in other countries have access to significantly more capable tools.

**Interviewer:** Can you give an example of where free tools fall short in your research writing?

**Participant:** When writing complex literature syntheses, free models often give superficial summaries and struggle with subtle syntactic transitions.

**Interviewer:** How do you compensate for the limitations of free AI systems?

**Participant:** It requires extensive manual editing and multiple rounds of proofreading, which significantly slows down manuscript completion.

**Interviewer:** What is your perspective on achieving SDG 4 in technologically constrained regions?

**Participant:** SDG 4 requires that high-quality digital learning tools are made accessible to all learners, rather than restricted by geographic and economic walls.
