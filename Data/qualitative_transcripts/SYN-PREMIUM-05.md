## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How do you perceive the global dialogue surrounding AI ethics and accessibility?

**Participant:** Global debates focus heavily on algorithmic bias, yet they often ignore the geopolitical exclusion that blocks entire academic populations from accessing tools at all.

**Interviewer:** In what ways does this exclusion translate into academic marginalization?

**Participant:** When top international conferences expect AI-assisted productivity, scholars without seamless access fall further behind in publication velocity and visibility.

**Interviewer:** How does using an advanced model change your manuscript drafting experience?

**Participant:** It provides nuanced lexical alternatives that help convey subtle academic arguments without oversimplifying the conceptual framework.

**Interviewer:** What psychological burden accompanies this technological divide?

**Participant:** A feeling of second-class academic status, knowing that simple infrastructural privileges taken for granted elsewhere require exhausting effort here.

**Interviewer:** Have you observed collaborative difficulties with international research groups?

**Participant:** Yes, when co-authors suggest using integrated AI collaborative platforms that are regionally blocked, it creates awkward communication friction.

**Interviewer:** What message would you share with international academic bodies?

**Participant:** Inclusivity statements should address technological embargoes and support open educational access across all geographic boundaries.
