## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How do international banking sanctions intersect with your academic research needs?

**Participant:** The total absence of international financial integration means we cannot subscribe to research tools or AI platforms through standard academic channels.

**Interviewer:** What strategies are required to maintain access under these conditions?

**Participant:** Scholars are forced to rely on complex intermediaries or informal networks, which are expensive, precarious, and lack consumer protection.

**Interviewer:** Once inside the platform, how does the premium tier assist with academic synthesis?

**Participant:** The model processes lengthy methodological descriptions and suggests syntactic restructuring while preserving disciplinary accuracy.

**Interviewer:** Does having premium access fully resolve the equity gap in research production?

**Participant:** No, because the underlying systemic exclusion remains. We are always one platform policy update away from losing our tools entirely.

**Interviewer:** How does this systemic barrier impact early-career researchers in your field?

**Participant:** It creates severe disparity within our domestic community, where only researchers with specific external connections can access cutting-edge tools.

**Interviewer:** How does this relate to the objectives of SDG 4 on equitable quality education?

**Participant:** SDG 4 cannot be realized globally if foundational digital knowledge tools remain walled off behind geopolitical banking barriers.
