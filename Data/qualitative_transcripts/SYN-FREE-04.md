## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** What emotions are most prominent when working with constrained free AI tools?

**Participant:** Frustration and anxiety. You see international demonstrations of state-of-the-art AI, but you are stuck with basic, restricted versions.

**Interviewer:** What specific writing limitations do you observe in free-tier outputs?

**Participant:** The vocabulary is often simplistic, repetitive, and lacks the syntactic variety needed for rigorous academic prose.

**Interviewer:** How do you navigate the inability to subscribe to premium alternatives?

**Participant:** You simply accept the constraint and spend hours manually rewriting and searching through dictionaries to elevate the text.

**Interviewer:** Does this additional effort affect your research productivity?

**Participant:** Substantially. What takes a researcher with premium tools a few minutes takes us hours of iterative manual correction.

**Interviewer:** How does this disparity affect early-stage scholars in your environment?

**Participant:** It discourages them from attempting high-impact international submissions because they feel structurally uncompetitive from the start.

**Interviewer:** What core principle should guide AI development for global education?

**Participant:** AI tools should be treated as shared human knowledge infrastructure rather than commercial commodities restricted by national boundaries.
