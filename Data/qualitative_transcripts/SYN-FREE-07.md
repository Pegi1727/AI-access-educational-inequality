## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How do structural sanctions impact your everyday academic capabilities?

**Participant:** Sanctions create a digital wall. We are excluded from commercial AI ecosystems simply because of our geographic location.

**Interviewer:** What happens when you rely solely on free AI tiers for research writing?

**Participant:** The outputs lack the contextual depth needed for high-level scholarly discussion, forcing us to spend double the time on manual revisions.

**Interviewer:** Have you experienced difficulties with account verifications on free platforms?

**Participant:** Yes, even free tiers frequently block registration from our regional phone numbers or IP ranges, requiring constant workarounds.

**Interviewer:** How does this affect your academic morale?

**Participant:** It generates chronic digital fatigue. You are exhausted before you even start the actual writing process.

**Interviewer:** How does this technological divide influence national research development?

**Participant:** It slows down overall institutional research output and widens the knowledge gap with international universities.

**Interviewer:** What is your vision for global educational policy under SDG 4?

**Participant:** International bodies must treat AI as a global public good that should be exempt from geopolitical and economic embargos.
