## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** What role do premium AI models play in bridging the language gap for international journal submissions?

**Participant:** They serve as a vital language interlocutor. Premium models allow non-native English scholars to express intricate theoretical arguments with syntactic precision.

**Interviewer:** Do you perceive an implicit standard in global publishing that penalizes researchers with limited tool access?

**Participant:** Absolutely. Reviewers frequently judge linguistic fluency as a proxy for research rigor, which places scholars from resource-limited environments at a persistent disadvantage.

**Interviewer:** How does the reliance on advanced AI affect your academic confidence?

**Participant:** It levels the playing field during drafting, but it also reinforces the uncomfortable reality that our institutional environment lacks direct institutional subscriptions.

**Interviewer:** What operational obstacles do you encounter even when you have access to a premium tier?

**Participant:** Authentication hurdles and IP restrictions frequently interrupt sessions, requiring constant re-verification and workaround management.

**Interviewer:** How does this experience connect to broader discussions of equity in higher education?

**Participant:** It demonstrates that technological advancements can inadvertently deepen educational inequality if marginalized scholars are systematically excluded from official adoption.

**Interviewer:** What changes would you hope to see in international educational policy?

**Participant:** Academic publishers and technology providers should recognize regional constraints and provide equitable research exemptions aligned with SDG 4 principles.
