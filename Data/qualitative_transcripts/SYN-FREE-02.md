## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** In what ways does limited AI access affect your ability to publish in international journals?

**Participant:** Manuscripts written with basic assistance often face harsher editorial scrutiny regarding English academic style, creating a barrier to entry.

**Interviewer:** Have you attempted to access premium AI versions to address this issue?

**Participant:** Yes, but without international payment cards, direct subscription is impossible, and informal third-party alternatives are far too expensive.

**Interviewer:** How does this situation influence your day-to-day motivation as a researcher?

**Participant:** It can be deeply demoralizing to realize that your scholarly contribution might be overlooked due to linguistic polishing barriers.

**Interviewer:** What differences do you notice between free model outputs and published academic discourse?

**Participant:** Free models lack lexical diversity and tend to use generic templates that do not reflect the sophisticated cohesion required by high-tier journals.

**Interviewer:** Do you discuss these technological disparities with academic peers?

**Participant:** We talk about it constantly; everyone in our department is searching for ways to access better tools without breaking limited budgets.

**Interviewer:** What policy recommendation would you emphasize to international bodies?

**Participant:** International academic organizations should advocate for open educational licenses so scholars are not marginalized based on nationality.
