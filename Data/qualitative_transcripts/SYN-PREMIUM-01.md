## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How does having access to premium AI tools influence your daily academic writing workflow?

**Participant:** The tools make revising complex scholarly paragraphs much faster, but maintaining access remains a constant struggle because direct payment channels are completely closed off to our banking cards.

**Interviewer:** When you encounter those payment restrictions, how does it affect your sense of participation in global academia?

**Participant:** It creates an underlying anxiety. Even though I have advanced models today, I always wonder if my account will be locked tomorrow simply because of where I am located.

**Interviewer:** In what ways do premium models specifically support your manuscript preparation compared to basic tools?

**Participant:** They help with nuanced phrasing, structural transitions, and vocabulary refinement that free tiers cannot handle with the same contextual depth.

**Interviewer:** Does the technical barrier to acquiring these tools create friction among your peers?

**Participant:** Yes, there is a clear divide between those who manage to secure external assistance for subscription renewals and those who must rely only on standard free tools.

**Interviewer:** How do you feel when international academic calls assume all researchers have seamless digital access?

**Participant:** It feels like our structural disadvantages are invisible. International journals expect high stylistic quality while ignoring the digital barriers we face to attain it.

**Interviewer:** Looking forward, what does sustainable educational access mean for researchers in constrained environments?

**Participant:** It means educational platforms and AI technologies should be decoupled from geopolitical borders so that quality education under SDG 4 is genuinely open to everyone.
