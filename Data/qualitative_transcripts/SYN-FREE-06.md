## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** What are the everyday technical hurdles of using free AI tools for your studies?

**Participant:** Frequent service cutoffs, prompt token caps, and sudden IP restrictions make it difficult to maintain an uninterrupted writing session.

**Interviewer:** Why not bypass these issues by acquiring a premium account?

**Participant:** Because our banking cards are disconnected from global payment systems, making official purchase impossible for regular researchers.

**Interviewer:** How do you feel when you read about cutting-edge AI breakthroughs?

**Participant:** There is an overwhelming sense of technological isolation, as if the global research community is advancing while we are left behind.

**Interviewer:** How does this affect your academic writing quality?

**Participant:** It limits the lexical sophistication and syntactic range of our initial drafts, demanding much more manual proofreading effort.

**Interviewer:** What strategies do you use to overcome these limitations?

**Participant:** We combine multiple free tools and cross-check outputs manually, though this is cumbersome and inefficient.

**Interviewer:** What does educational equity mean in the age of generative AI?

**Participant:** Equity means ensuring that access to advanced cognitive and linguistic tools is not determined by geopolitical circumstances.
