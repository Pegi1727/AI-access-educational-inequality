## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How do you evaluate the overall impact of geopolitical isolation on academic productivity?

**Participant:** Geopolitical isolation acts as an invisible tax on scholarship, forcing researchers to invest excessive time and money into basic digital connectivity.

**Interviewer:** When you have premium AI access, what specific writing improvements do you notice?

**Participant:** The writing exhibits better discourse cohesion, varied lexical choices, and smoother transitions between theoretical concepts.

**Interviewer:** What happens when your access is suddenly interrupted by platform restrictions?

**Participant:** Drafting stalls immediately, and you are forced to pivot back to less capable free alternatives, which degrades workflow consistency.

**Interviewer:** How does this affect your perception of international educational equity?

**Participant:** It highlights how market-driven AI distribution models exacerbate existing global inequalities rather than democratizing knowledge.

**Interviewer:** Do you feel that academic evaluation systems adequately account for these challenges?

**Participant:** Not at all. Performance metrics in global academia treat all researchers as if they operate with identical access and infrastructure.

**Interviewer:** What would be the most impactful step toward educational fairness under SDG 4?

**Participant:** Creating global academic exemptions that permit educational and scientific use of AI technologies regardless of regional financial sanctions.
