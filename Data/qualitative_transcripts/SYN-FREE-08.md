## Transcript Dialogue (Synthetic Role-Play)

**Interviewer:** How does operating under free AI access affect your confidence in academic publishing?

**Participant:** It makes you self-conscious about writing quality. You know that competitors with premium tools can produce polished prose in a fraction of the time.

**Interviewer:** What prevents your university from providing premium licenses to students and staff?

**Participant:** Institutional procurement is impossible due to global banking restrictions and lack of direct commercial agreements with technology firms.

**Interviewer:** How does the free tier perform when handling complex theoretical concepts?

**Participant:** It tends to flatten conceptual nuances, using generic terminology that fails to capture specialized academic arguments.

**Interviewer:** What emotional toll does this technological disparity take on researchers?

**Participant:** A pervasive sense of unfairness and frustration, knowing that intellectual potential is hindered by non-academic geopolitical factors.

**Interviewer:** How do researchers in your setting demonstrate resilience?

**Participant:** Through persistent peer support, collaborative editing groups, and continuous dedication despite resource shortages.

**Interviewer:** What key message regarding SDG 4 would you like to convey?

**Participant:** Quality education must include equitable technological access; without it, the digital divide will only perpetuate academic marginalization.
