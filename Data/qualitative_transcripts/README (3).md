# SDG 4 AI Accessibility Study — Synthetic Qualitative Exemplar Repository

This repository provides **15 English synthetic interview exemplars** designed to demonstrate qualitative coding structures, thematic mapping, and repository organization for research exploring artificial intelligence accessibility, educational inequality, and academic resilience in sanctioned environments (SDG 4: Quality Education).

---

## ⚠️ PROMINENT NOTICE: SYNTHETIC / RECONSTRUCTED EXEMPLARS (NOT RAW DATA)

- **No raw interview transcripts** were present in the source materials or analytical datasets.
- The 15 Markdown files in this directory are **synthetic and reconstructed exemplars** generated solely for demonstration, codebook illustration, and repository packaging.
- These synthetic transcripts **must NOT be cited, interpreted, or presented as empirical participant data or quotes from real individuals**.
- No quantitative metric (Lexical_TTR, Syntactic_Cscore, Cohesion_Score) is linked to these synthetic records, and no participant-level linkage exists between these files and participants `P01–P60` in the quantitative dataset.

---

## 1. Study & Context Summary

- **Research Context:** United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG 4 / Quality Education), focusing on AI tool accessibility, linguistic/educational disparities, and academic resilience among researchers operating in geopolitically constrained and sanctioned environments.
- **Available Source Files in Workspace:**
  1. `main doc edited.docx` — Primary manuscript on AI accessibility, educational inequality, and resilience.
  2. `Supplementary_Materials_Final.docx` — Supplementary documentation, descriptive statistics, and inferential tables.
  3. `Appendices.docx` — Dataset definitions, variable breakdowns, and analytical workflows.
  4. `SDG4_Quant_Data.csv` — Quantitative dataset containing 60 records (`P01–P60`) evaluating `Lexical_TTR`, `Syntactic_Cscore`, and `Cohesion_Score` across Premium and Free conditions.
  5. `SDG4_Codebook.csv` — Variable metadata dictionary for the quantitative study.
  6. `SDG4_Qual_Codes.csv` — Variable dictionary file (Note: this file duplicated `SDG4_Codebook.csv` structure and contained metadata definitions rather than qualitative transcript text).
  7. `SDG4_Geopolitical_Divide_Dataset.xlsx` — Excel master dataset containing study variables and group records.

## 3. High-Level Qualitative Themes & Code Mapping

All synthetic dialogues and illustrative coding sections strictly adhere to the three qualitative themes identified in the study framework:

1. **Systemic Exclusion:**
   - Barriers arising from international banking disconnects, lack of foreign payment cards, institutional procurement blockades, and regional digital embargoes.
2. **Academic Marginalization:**
   - Perceptions of operating as peripheral scholars, linguistic gatekeeping in international publishing, unequal academic velocity, and feelings of second-class academic citizenship.
3. **Digital Frustration:**
   - Acute digital anxiety, "AI FOMO" (fear of missing out on state-of-the-art models), platform authentication interruptions, prompt rate limits, and cognitive fatigue caused by continuous technical troubleshooting.

---

## 4. What Is Intentionally Absent

To prevent misleading claims or empirical overreach, the following elements are **strictly omitted** from this synthetic dataset:
- Participant names, demographic characteristics, institutional affiliations, and geographical locations.
- Real ethical approval codes, consent forms, or specific interview administration dates.
- Specific VPN names, payment bypass techniques, private intermediaries, or unauthorized technical instructions.
- Participant-level quantitative scores (e.g., TTR, C-score, Cohesion ratings) or participant ID overlap with `P01–P60`.
- Extraneous qualitative themes not grounded in the source study framework.
